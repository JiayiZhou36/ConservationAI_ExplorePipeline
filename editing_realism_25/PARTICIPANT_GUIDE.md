# Help us evaluate edited wildlife photographs

We are studying image edits used to test animal-detection software. We would
like to know how natural the edited photographs look to people. You do not
need any technical background.

You will see 25 pairs. The original photograph is on the left and an edited
version is on the right. In some images, an animal region was edited to replace
it with another object. In others, the background was changed while the animal
regions were kept.

Use the original to identify what changed. Rate only the edited image. Look at
the texture, lighting, edges and how well the changed area fits the rest of the
photograph. You are judging appearance, not whether you like the change or
would expect that scene in the wild.

Choose one answer:

5. **Looks completely natural.** I cannot notice any signs of editing.
4. **Looks mostly natural.** The changed area looks natural, with only small signs of editing.
3. **Looks partly natural.** Some parts look natural, but others look edited.
2. **Looks mostly edited.** Most of the changed area looks edited, with only small parts looking natural.
1. **Looks clearly edited.** The changed area does not look natural.

You can also choose **I cannot judge this image**.

Click an image to enlarge it. Use Save and next and Previous to save or review
your answers. Review the completion page and click Submit study when ready.
Answers cannot be changed after submission.

Participation is voluntary. No name or email is requested. Please complete the
study once and use the same browser if you return to an unfinished session.
Thank you for helping us evaluate these images.
